|Pyjamas & luxury sleepwear|1|The reader's launch request, verbatim ethos: "if I wanted a luxury set — what do people lust over? Makers of genuine quality, not brand names for brand's own sake." Teach the hierarchy from cloth up: two-fold Sea Island cotton and Swiss batiste vs ordinary poplin, silk by momme weight (and why below ~19 momme is lingerie-thin), Irish linen and cotton-cashmere for the cold-room sleeper — then construction (mother-of-pearl buttons, piping, side gussets, French seams, hand-finished buttonholes). The makers to run down honestly: the Jermyn Street/London bench — Derek Rose (the category's standing reference), Emma Willis (cut in her Gloucester workshop, the true bespoke route), Turnbull & Asser and Budd (bespoke PJs to measure), New & Lingwood (the lounge-lizard grail gowns); the Continent — Zimmerli of Switzerland (the quiet "best cotton garments in the world" claim, verify), Schostal of Rome (the old-Rome hold-out), Charvet Paris (the silk grail); the women's-led silk tier — Olivia von Halle (22-momme), Asceno, La Perla honestly assessed; the newer natural-materials wave — Tekla (Copenhagen), Piglet in Bed (linen), General Sleep; and the value-of-quality floor — Sunspel, Hanro. Who actually weaves/sews for whom (white-label truths — many "heritage" PJs come from the same few factories; name what can be verified, hold what can't). Communities to mine: Permanent Style (Simon Crompton has covered bespoke pyjamas), Styleforum's sleepwear/shirting threads, r/BuyItForLife, r/femalefashionadvice silk threads. Prices tiered honestly (entry-of-quality ~S$150-250 Sunspel/Hanro → sweet spot ~S$400-700 Derek Rose/Zimmerli → bespoke/grail S$1,000+ Emma Willis/Charvet/OvH) with the what-you're-paying-for test at each step. SINGAPORE ANGLE, load-bearing: a 28°C bedroom changes the answer — airweight batiste, linen and true lightweight silk over flannel and heavy piped cotton; who ships here / local stockists. Care as the buy-once case (silk laundering honestly; why the batiste set outlives ten fast-fashion sets). Sceptic: the logo-tax tier (monogram-first "luxury" PJs), whether silk is practical at all in the tropics, and the honest line where price stops buying cloth and starts buying packaging.|

|Audio cables & hi-fi accessories — the sceptic special|2|The desk's honesty benchmark, run as a full-dress deep dive INTO a category that is substantially theatre — and the reader's pick. The point: The Atelier proves it can tell genuine craft from ritual, in the reader's own hobby (he runs serious hi-fi). Structure the split honestly: (a) WHERE MONEY IS REAL — competent construction and materials (proper geometry/shielding for noise-prone runs, quality terminations: genuine Neutrik/Switchcraft/Amphenol connectors, silver-solder workmanship), professional/studio-grade makers who charge for build not mysticism (Mogami, Canare, Belden as the pro-audio truth; Blue Jeans Cable's published test-report ethos as the model citizen), honest custom-cable ateliers doing beautiful sleeving/lengths at sane prices; plus adjacent accessories where engineering is real (isolation done right vs done ridiculous, mains filtering honestly bounded). (b) WHERE IT IS THEATRE — the four- and five-figure interconnect/speaker-cable tier: directional arrows on copper, cryogenic treatment, cable lifters, burn-in narratives; the measurement community's receipts (Audio Science Review's cable measurements, the classic blind-test literature incl. the famous coat-hanger test, ABX history) laid against the subjectivist press that reviews $10k cables without measurement. Name names carefully and factually: report measured findings and documented claims, attribute community sentiment, never assert fraud — the sceptic desk's own evenhandedness rules apply to the sceptic special. (c) THE HUMAN READ — why smart people hear differences (auditory expectation effects, level-matching traps), written with respect, not sneering; the hobby's own defenders given their steel-man. (d) THE BUY-ONCE VERDICT — what a rational high-end system actually warrants per connection type (analog IC, speaker, USB/digital, power), with the honest ceiling in dollars, and where the leftover budget genuinely improves sound (room treatment, speakers). Communities: ASR forum, r/audiophile vs r/BuyItForLife, Gearspace/pro-audio threads, Head-Fi's cable wars as anthropology. SG angle: local pro-audio suppliers vs the boutique-cable boutiques.|
